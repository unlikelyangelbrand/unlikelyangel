Angels Among Us

A Community Wellness Experience • Dec 19, 2026 • Downtown LA

Moving beyond standard care package hand-outs, Angels Among Us transforms Downtown LA (5th & San Pedro) into an uplifting, block-party-style experience designed to serve 100–150 unhoused individuals with total dignity and respect.

1. Body

Hot chef-prepared meals, water, seasonal care packages, hygienic essentials, and clean apparel.

2. Mind & Dignity

On-site mobile haircuts, mobile shower access, and tailored styling to help guests feel renewed.

3. Spirit

Community connection, voluntary prayer/listening stations, photo portraits, and genuine conversation.
